# EDA interpretation and cleaning addendum

The audit supports data-quality and evaluation decisions for the
[vendor-restricted visibility study](research_scope.md). It does not identify
a safe feature-reduction rule or a vendor capability profile. This follow-up
addresses descriptive statistics, temporal interpretation, feature associations
and the cleaning implementation before the teammate handoff.

## Populations and reproducibility

The reproducible summary is results/eda_summary_2026-11-24.json, generated by
src/reliability/eda_summary.py. It verifies all 50 input hashes and the existing
cohort/window/split/timing parents before and after analysis. It keeps numeric
missing and invalid values distinct, uses sample standard deviation and linear
percentiles at position (n-1)p. Numeric validity does not establish physical
validity. The 17 audited metric columns include constants; they are not a
selected model feature set. Category-specific statistics and 40 primary-file
BLER associations are retained in the JSON.

These are descriptive, pooled-report associations, not model results or
independent-capture estimates. Whole-database/class summaries are disclosed

Changing cohort composition is not a paired robustness experiment.

## Descriptive statistics and feature interpretation

The combined notebook now displays count, missing percentage, invalid count,
mean, sample SD, minimum, 5th/25th/50th/75th/95th percentiles, maximum and IQR
for full, primary and primary-training populations. The training examples
below show why means and correlation alone are insufficient.

| Stored metric | Training median | Training IQR | Training mean | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| dlBler | 0.016799 | 0.104259 | 0.150991 | Mean substantially exceeds median; upper-tail values affect the average |
| ulBler | 0.049566 | 0.121399 | 0.185551 | Summarize spread and tail behavior as well as central tendency |
| dlBytes | 41,021,571 | 275,648,860 | 550,481,381.785 | Broad scale/skew in stored values; counter semantics and rate conversion remain unresolved |

| rsrq | -11 | 0.5 | -10.936212 | A stored minimum of -32 lies well below its 5th percentile of -12; flag for semantic review, not automatic clipping |

No physical units, thresholds or model transformations are inferred from
these descriptive values. Constant ri/pmi metrics remain visible in the new
summary. Existing audits also identify constant cellid/in_sync metadata.
Identifiers and labels stay as lineage/truth metadata rather than predictors.

Across 40 primary files, 38 have defined BLER correlation, ranging from

Category and file composition therefore matter. The training DoS subset has
only one primary source-file group; its association is not representative of
all DoS captures. These results do not establish causality, measurement
interchangeability or safe removal of either uplink/downlink BLER. Nitendra
can use this as motivation for a reviewed training-only comparison, not as
an adopted feature-selection rule. Statistical association also does not
establish which measurements any vendor exposes.

## Temporal interpretation

The approved primary cohort covers 87 descriptive source/UE streams. All have
median positive adjacent step 1,000 raw units, with no backward/zero steps.
Of 43,617 comparable adjacent pairs, 40,075 have step 1,000. The largest
adjacent step is 143,993 raw units. These statements use physical source order
and existing timing diagnostics, not global database sorting or inferred runs.

The 4,332 complete ten-report windows have median/IQR span 9,000/0 raw units,
but spans range from 8,998 to 150,147. Equal report counts therefore do not
imply equal elapsed spans. The 74 retained tails span 0 to 8,000 raw units;
a one-report tail can legitimately have zero span. Larger steps alone do not
identify packet loss, stale data, attack onset or a reporting schedule.

The legacy root EDA's UTC/date plots assume Unix milliseconds. They are now
explicitly labeled historical/provisional and do not override EP-3. The 1,540
secondary rows remain outside primary temporal analysis. Calendar dates,
per-second rates, freshness duration and time-to-detection remain deferred.
